Frequently Asked Questions

I'm new to Notion and Apple Shortcuts. Is Wellness Hub OS beginner friendly?

Yes! While there is an initial setup process to personalize the system, Wellness Hub OS includes a comprehensive setup guide that walks you through every step. Once everything is configured, day-to-day use is designed to be simple, intuitive, and easy to maintain.

Do I need Apple Shortcuts to use Wellness Hub OS?

No. The Notion template can be used on its own. However, the included Apple Shortcuts unlock guided workouts, beauty routines, medication tracking, routine automation, and other advanced features.

Can I customize the system?

Absolutely! Every database, routine, workout, medication, appointment, and beauty routine can be personalized to fit your lifestyle.

Is there a setup process?

Yes. Wellness Hub OS includes a detailed setup guide that walks you through configuring both the Notion template and the Apple Shortcuts. Once everything is set up, daily use is simple.

What devices does this work on?

The Notion template works on any device that supports Notion. The included shortcuts require Apple Shortcuts and are designed for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
